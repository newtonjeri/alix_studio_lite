 9. LIMITS OF THIS CAPTURE
 --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   - lshw was run without sudo. It said so itself, at the start and at the end:
     "you should run this program as super-user" and "output may be incomplete or
     inaccurate". A complete listing:  sudo lshw -short
   - lshw -short cuts long descriptions at its column width. Three were cut and
     are written out in full above: "RTL8822CE 802.11ac PCIe Wireless Netwo"
     (Network adapter), "... HDA HDMI/DP,pcm" and "... APE Headset Jac" (Jack).
   - Not captured: the JetPack release, the disks' partitions and free space,
     the network addresses, the power mode, the cameras on USB.

   To capture everything again, on the Jetson:
       sudo lshw -short
       hostnamectl
       cat /etc/nv_tegra_release
       lsblk
       df -h /
       ip -4 addr
       sudo nvpmodel -q
   or, for what ALIX Studio Lite needs of this computer, in one command that
   changes nothing:
